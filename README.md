### Project Overview
This project analyzes Coca-Cola retail sales data for 2022–2023 using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to evaluate sales performance, profitability, retailer contributions, regional trends, and beverage brand performance. Interactive dashboards and PivotTables were used to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights and strategic recommendations.

### Business Problem
Coca-Cola aims to understand how sales performance varies across regions, retailers, and beverage brands in order to identify growth opportunities, improve profitability, and support data-driven business decisions.

### Objectives
- Analyze total sales and operating profit.
- Evaluate beverage brand performance.
- Compare regional and retailer performance.
- Identify sales trends between 2022 and 2023.
- Provide business recommendations.

### Key Insights
- Coca-Cola generated the highest total sales among all beverage brands.
- The West region recorded the strongest sales performance.
- Revenue increased from 2022 to 2023.
- Retail sales were concentrated among a few high-performing retailers.
- Operating margins varied across beverage brands.

### Recommendation
Based on the analysis, Coca-Cola should continue prioritizing its top-performing beverage brands, retailers, and regions while investigating areas with lower profitability despite strong sales. Optimizing pricing strategies, improving operational efficiency, and replicating successful practices from high-performing markets can help drive sustainable growth. Additionally, leveraging historical sales trends for inventory planning and targeted marketing campaigns will support better decision-making, improve resource allocation, and enhance overall business performance.
